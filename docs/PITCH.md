# AstroMed: slide outline (6 slides)

## 1. The problem
- Missions are getting longer and farther: Artemis to the Moon, then Mars.
- NASA's five hazards: radiation, isolation and confinement, distance from Earth, altered gravity, hostile closed environments.
- On Mars, a message to Earth takes 4–24 minutes each way. Astronauts must assess themselves and act first.

## 2. AstroMed in one sentence
A visual health self-assessment tool that tells an astronaut what their readings mean in their environment, and what to do, how soon, and with whom.

## 3. How it works (one astronaut's day)
1. Log vitals, sleep, exercise, food and mood (under a minute), or do a guided checkup.
2. See each indicator against your own normal range, color coded with icon and label.
3. Report any problem in plain words, with a photo; AstroMed matches NASA's condition list and flags emergencies instantly.
4. Get the right action for your distance: self-care, next check-in, flight surgeon today, or act now with your crew.
5. Review My summary and take its questions to the flight surgeon.

## 4. Grounded in NASA evidence
- About 80 conditions from NASA-STD-3001 Vol. 1 Table E.2-2, grouped under the five hazards, and all 17 of NASA HRP's spaceflight risks mapped to where AstroMed tracks them.
- Partner agency evidence: CSA's radiation monitoring work (neutrons are about 30% of ISS radiation).
- Every tip and threshold cites a NASA page: crew health standards, HRP evidence reports.
- NASA's own energy equation for nutrition; NASA's 2.5 L water minimum; the 600 mSv career radiation limit.
- Personal baselines from the MMASH dataset until the astronaut has their own.

## 5. Designed for astronauts
- Clickable anatomical body map; interactive charts; gamification (XP, ranks, streaks).
- Works on phones and offline; installs to the home screen.
- Safety first: no diagnosis, no medicine recommendations, emergency phrases always escalate, 73 automated safety tests.
- Private by design: each person sees only their own data; optional bring-your-own AI keeps the key on the device.

## 6. What's next
- Push reminders and direct sensor or smartwatch sync.
- Crew medical officer view for the whole crew.
- Validation with flight surgeons and analog missions (Antarctic stations, HERA, CHAPEA).
